## ExpandableExerciseTimer

A custom workout timer for iOS, macOS, and visionOS. Build routines by mixing time-based and rep-based exercises, dial in rest periods between sets, and get audio alerts when it's time to move on. Born out of frustration with the quirks of browser-based timers, it's grown into a full-featured tool I use every day — for workouts *and* as a slow-eating timer. The iOS version now includes live Health metrics from a paired Apple Watch — current heart rate and calorie burn during your workouts.

## Bodyweight WorkoutRandomizer

Can't decide what to do today? This app takes the decision fatigue out of bodyweight training — pick your exercises, set your work and rest intervals, and let it shuffle the rest. Great for days when you want variety without the planning overhead. Grew out of a Zone 2 cardio workaround I built when my ankle was acting up and has been evolving ever since. Like EET, the iOS version shows live heart rate and calorie burn from a paired Apple Watch.

[![Download on the App Store](https://tools.applemediaservices.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/en-us?size=250x83)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bodyweight-workoutrandomizer/id6757230155)

- 💻 [GitHub repo](https://github.com/bigdoodr/WorkoutRandomizer)

## PomoLedger

A Pomodoro-style focus timer with a twist: at the end of each session, you're prompted to summarize what you worked on before choosing your break length. Those recaps accumulate into a file you can share with your favorite AI to help write reports, pull together a weekly summary, or just keep yourself honest about where the time went. Supports manual or automatic archiving on a weekly or monthly schedule, and optional calendar logging of focus blocks.

- 🧪 [TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uvFhVjdW)

## CursorIlluminator

A macOS utility that puts a spotlight on your cursor, making it easy to find on big screens and multi-monitor setups. Simple, lightweight, and does exactly one thing well. Due to macOS sandbox restrictions, it can't live in the App Store — but it's free and open source on GitHub.

- 💻 [GitHub repo](https://github.com/bigdoodr/CursorIlluminator)
- 📦 [Download v1.0.0](https://github.com/bigdoodr/CursorIlluminator/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
- *(Not available on the App Store due to sandbox restrictions)*
